Noam Bettan has a very distinct, charismatic look that blends classic Mediterranean features with a modern style.
### General Features & Style
 * **The Vibe:** He projects a cool, effortless confidence on stage. Journalists often describe his look as a classic "Tel Aviv hipster" style—chic, modern, and sharp.
 * **Facial Features:** He has dark, expressive eyes, well-defined eyebrows, and a warm smile. He keeps his dark hair relatively short and styled, paired with a neatly trimmed, sharp beard and mustache.
 * **Build:** He has a tall, athletic, and commanding stage presence, standing confidently under the spotlight.
### His Eurovision Stage Look
For his performance of "Michelle" in Vienna, his wardrobe matched the sleek, moody pop energy of the song. He wore an eye-catching, contemporary outfit featuring **black leather**, which gave him a bit of an edgy, rock-pop star appeal while remaining incredibly polished.
